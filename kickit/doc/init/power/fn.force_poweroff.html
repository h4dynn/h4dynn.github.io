Skip normal poweroff routine, and shutdown immediately (not recommended!)

§Errors

This function is a frontend to nix::sys::reboot::reboot. Refer to its error documentation.

§Safety

This function is marked unsafe due to its lack of a safe poweroff routine, skipping straight to the basic Linux reboot syscall.

§Data loss

In the regular poweroff routine, each filesystem is unmounted, allowing them to sync their data properly & in a safe manner. As this is skipped, they have no opportunity to do so, leading to potential data loss.

This risk is mitigated by journaling, which is performed by the vast majority of modern filesystems1. Journaling filesystems keep track of uncommitted modifications, before they complete, reducing the risk of corruption after being brought back online.

However, after being brought back online, if there was any data being actively written to the disk, it may become lost or corrupted.

TL;DR: Journaling will save your filesystem majority of the time, but don’t rely on it exclusively, and ideally have a backup
